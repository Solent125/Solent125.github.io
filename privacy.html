Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy matters to us

We collect as little information as possible, and only to help you: to answer your questions, to understand what you find useful on our website, and to keep making it better for you. We never sell your data.

When you get in touch

If you send us a message through our contact form or by email, we receive your name, email address, phone number (if you give it) and your message. We use these details only to reply to you and to help with whatever you've asked about. Our contact form is handled by Formspree, which passes your message securely to our inbox.

When you browse our website

If you choose to accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics to see, in general terms, which pages are most read and how people find us. This shows us which topics are most helpful to you, so we can write more of what you need. It does not tell us who you are. If you click Reject, no analytics cookies are set, and the website works just the same.

Cookies

If you accept, Google Analytics sets a small number of cookies that help us count visits; they last for up to 2 years. We also remember your cookie choice in your browser, so we don't ask you on every page. You can change your mind at any time: open cookie settings.

Who sees your information

Only the services that help us run this website: Formspree (contact form), Google (analytics, only if you accept) and GitHub (website hosting). Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages only as long as we need them to help you and for our business records. Website analytics data is kept for 14 months.

You're in control

You can ask us at any time to see the information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it, or to withdraw your consent to cookies. Just email info@solentconsultancy.com and we'll take care of it.

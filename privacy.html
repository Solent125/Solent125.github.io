Who we are

This website is run by Solent Consultancy Limited, a company registered in England & Wales (Company No. 15283739), Unit 12, Basepoint, Harts Farm Way, Havant, PO9 1HS. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this notice. If you have any questions about your data, email us at info@solentconsultancy.com.

What we collect and why

When you contact us. If you use our contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address, phone number (if you give it) and your message. We use this only to reply to you and to discuss the services you are interested in. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Our contact form is processed by Formspree, which forwards your message to our inbox. Formspree may process data outside the UK, under appropriate safeguards.

When you visit the website. If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are viewed and how people found us. This is reported to us in aggregate and is not used to identify you. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Cookies

We only set analytics cookies if you click Accept analytics in the cookie banner. If you click Reject, or make no choice, no analytics cookies are set.

  • _ga, _ga_* (Google Analytics) — distinguish visitors and sessions so we can count visits. Expire after up to 2 years.

We also store your cookie choice in your browser so we don't ask you on every page. You can change your mind at any time: open cookie settings.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers that help us run this website — Formspree (contact form), Google (analytics, only with your consent) and GitHub (website hosting) — or where the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only for as long as we need them to deal with your request and for our business records. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To make a request, email info@solentconsultancy.com.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
